# TND6396 — SiC: From Challenging Material to Robust Reliability

> **Original**: TND6396/D Rev. 2, March 2024
> **Author**: Catherine De Keukeleire (onsemi Oudenaarde, Belgium)
> **Category**: White Paper — onsemi Quality & Reliability Methodology for SiC
> **Position**: Primary reference for Part III §D (Reliability)

## 1. Abstract

Silicon Carbide (SiC) is a next-generation semiconductor for high-power electronic devices, with excellent physical properties — **high electron saturation drift velocity**, **high thermal conductivity**, and **high breakdown field**. SiC-based systems deliver **lower loss and faster switching in a smaller area** than Si MOSFETs.

Building on decades of Si know-how, onsemi has continually developed qualification methodologies tailored to the **unique challenges introduced by SiC**. This white paper covers:

- onsemi's **end-to-end Quality & Reliability methodology** from design to mass production
- SiC-specific evaluation flow centered on **Gate Oxide Integrity (GOI)**
- Latest research on **Cryogenic BTI · Body Diode Degradation · Dynamic Stress**

## 3. White-Paper 5-Step Methodology

```
Control → Improve → Test & Screen → Characterize → Qualify & Extract Models
```

| Step | Example applied to GOI |
|---|---|
| **Control** | Control Plan, SPC, Process FMEA |
| **Improve** | Reduce substrate / epi defects, metallic contamination, particles |
| **Test & Screen** | Substrate Scan + multi-step in-line inspection + Wafer level + Burn-in + Die Sort + Dynamic PAT + 100 % automated outgoing inspection |
| **Characterize** | Compare Q_BD (≈ 50 × Si) — Weibull-tail analysis |
| **Qualify & Extract Models** | Identify conduction mechanism (TAT / FN), Arrhenius + E-model, 21 V · 20 yr · 5 ppm |

## 4. Key Conclusions

| Area | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| **Gate Oxide Q_BD** | onsemi planar SiC intrinsic $Q_{BD}$ ≈ Si × 50 (5 mA/cm², Forward Bias Gate, RT) |
| **TDDB Lifetime** | At 175 °C / < 9 MV/cm, **20-year lifetime at $V_{gs}$ = 21 V to a 5-ppm criterion** |
| **BTI** | UF-BTI + CET Map + Comphy separates **fast application component + slow long-term component**; measurement range extended to cryogenic |
| **Body Diode** | Individual BPD mapping + heavily-doped epi buffer + explicit SOA = **practically zero impact at the 1200 V class** |
| **Dynamic Gate Stress** | onsemi 1200 V 80 mΩ outperforms three other vendors under −8 V ↔ +20 V 50 % duty |
